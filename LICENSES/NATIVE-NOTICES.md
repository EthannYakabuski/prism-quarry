# Native integrations

Original Prism Quarry adapter code is Apache-2.0. It derives from the same owner's Popline Park / The Final Outlaw adapter and retains its offline Play Games journal and safe-inset calculations.

- Godot Play Games Services **3.4.0**, MIT, from the supplied `GodotPlayGameServices` package through the reviewed Popline Park copy. Upstream: https://github.com/godot-sdk-integrations/godot-play-game-services . Notice: `GodotPlayGameServices-MIT.txt`, also retained inside the addon. Actual debug/release Android AARs are included.
- Poing Godot AdMob **5.1.0**, MIT, from the supplied `admob_plugin/poing-godot-admob` package through the reviewed Popline Park copy. Upstream: https://github.com/poingstudios/godot-admob-plugin . Notice: `admob-MIT.txt`, also retained inside the addon. Actual core and ads debug/release AARs are included; mediation libraries are absent.
- Native Poing Android template source receipt: https://github.com/poingstudios/godot-admob-plugin/releases/download/v5.1.0/android-template-v4.5.1.zip ; source archive SHA-256 `f7fdf644d50c0e231d8f02ae044057e489d0bcafcf50e908a4858477e7ee05ee`, recorded in the owner's shared platform source notes. Individual vendored AAR digests are emitted by `platform/tests/validate_platform.py`.
- Godot **4.5.1**, MIT: https://godotengine.org/license/ . `Godot-MIT.txt`, `Godot-third-party-licenses.json` and `Godot-third-party-copyrights.json` were extracted from the installed official engine's `Engine.get_license_text()`, `Engine.get_license_info()` and `Engine.get_copyright_info()` APIs. Android source/build binaries come from the installed official 4.5.1 templates and are generated build inputs, not game code. Preserve these notices when distributing engine builds.

Reviewed local addon changes: the Play Games exporter supports blank disabled game IDs and skips Android checks for Web; predeclared runtime autoloads are preserved; AdMob does not automatically download/install iOS binaries and keeps the sample app ID explicit. Unused sample scenes, translations, font, sample music, downloads and C# examples are omitted. Third-party notices in retained sources remain intact. Google SDK dependencies are resolved by Gradle and retain their respective terms; their presence is not evidence of owner backend setup or release approval.
