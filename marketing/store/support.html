Need a hand in the quarry?

The owner must supply a working support email and public support URL before publication. This draft does not send a support request.

Game controls

Drag the drill puck to aim and release to shoot. Read the predicted path before you commit. Use the visible pause and recall controls during play. After results, choose the next chamber or retry.

Progress and sound

Your progress is stored locally. Clearing app data or browser site data can remove it. Before reporting a problem, record the build version and describe what happened; do not clear your data as a troubleshooting step if you want to preserve it.

Music, sound effects and feedback controls are available in settings. Browser sound may need a click or tap before it starts.

Web preview

The Web build requires WebGL 2. Native Android ads and Play Games are unavailable in this preview. Browser compatibility and a running loading screen are not evidence that gameplay has passed device testing.

What to include in a bug report

Include your device model, operating-system version, app or build version, the chamber or menu involved, and the shortest steps that reproduce the issue. A screenshot can help. Never include passwords, signing keys or payment details.
