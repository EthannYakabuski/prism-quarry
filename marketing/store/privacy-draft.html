Prism Quarry privacy policy draft

This is a development disclosure draft, not the final production policy. The publishing entity, privacy contact, final SDK disclosures, retention practices and public policy address must be confirmed before release. No effective production date is asserted.

Local game progress

Prism Quarry stores game settings, chamber progress, ore, miner XP, upgrades, cosmetics, achievement progress, local records and claim history on the device or in the browser's site storage. The save is used to continue your game and avoid duplicate rewards. The development game has no publisher account registration or custom cloud-save service.

Local data remains until you remove the application's data, uninstall where the operating system removes that data, or clear the browser's site storage. Device backups may be managed by the operating system. The final production backup configuration and deletion instructions must be verified by the publisher.

Advertising on Android

The Android project includes Google Mobile Ads through the Poing AdMob integration and is configured for test advertising during development. Third-party advertising services may process device or other identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, app interactions and diagnostic information. Actual data handling depends on the SDK version, consent choices and production configuration. The publisher must reconcile the shipped SDK's disclosure guidance with the final Data safety answers.

Consent and privacy-choice controls must be configured and tested for the markets in which the game is released. A cancelled, failed or unavailable rewarded ad does not earn a continuation. The Web build has no native Android advertising service and does not simulate a reward.

Google Play Games

The Android integration can submit achievement progress and leaderboard scores when the owner has configured valid service identifiers and the service is available. Production mappings are disabled in the development package. The publisher must verify authentication, account identifiers, disclosure and deletion handling for the final integration. Google controls data associated with its own services under its applicable terms and policy.

Data sharing and security

The current game does not implement a publisher-operated analytics or account backend. This does not establish that native third-party SDKs collect no data. No claim of zero collection, zero sharing or encrypted local saves is made. The final policy must identify the publisher, service providers, purposes, security practices and any data transfers applicable to the released build.

Retention, deletion and contact

Local game data can be removed through device app-data controls or browser site-data controls. This resets progress. Any service-managed data must be addressed using the service's applicable account controls and the final publisher support process.
